Insider Threat Detection System

Insider threats come from individuals within an organization—such as employees, former employees, contractors, or business associates—who have inside information concerning the organization's security practices, data, and computer systems. These individuals can misuse their access to harm the organization intentionally or unintentionally. The harm can come in various forms, including theft of intellectual property, data breaches, and sabotage of computer systems.

The Insider Threat Detection System is designed to identify and mitigate potential insider threats before they can cause damage. Utilizing a combination of advanced analytics, machine learning, and user behavior analysis, the system monitors for unusual activity that could indicate a threat. By analyzing patterns and detecting anomalies, the system helps protect sensitive information and critical infrastructure from the risks posed by insider threats, ensuring the security and integrity of organizational assets.
